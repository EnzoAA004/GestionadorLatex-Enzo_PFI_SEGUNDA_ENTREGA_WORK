\begin{Abstract}
This Final Engineering Project designed and developed a software prototype to support the structural analysis of lumbar magnetic resonance imaging. The system integrates image processing, AI-assisted anatomical segmentation in sagittal and axial planes, geometric measurements, overlay visualization, and an editable structured output for professional review. Evaluation on the retained SPIDER sagittal split yielded a macro Dice score of 0.893, above the project threshold. The axial module, evaluated on the Sudirman and Al-Kafri dataset, achieved an official macro Dice score of 0.679 and did not meet that threshold; it is therefore treated as a complementary candidate rather than an established baseline. The product also integrates review, persistence, and export workflows, supported by documented technical tests and two qualitative professional review sessions. This evidence is not clinical validation: system measurements or diagnostic outputs were not compared with professionals on shared studies. The project demonstrates technical feasibility within a limited academic scope, while clinical accuracy, performance in routine practice, and observed commercial viability remain unproven.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} lumbar magnetic resonance imaging; medical image segmentation; artificial intelligence; anatomical measurements; professional review.
\end{Abstract}
